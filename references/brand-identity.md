| Name | Hex | Usage |

### Colour Rules
- Sidebar/nav: Always Brand Black background
- Logo on dark bg: Brand Beige or Brand Copper
- Text on light bg: Brand Dark
- Primary CTA buttons: Brand Coffee fill, white text
- Secondary CTA: Brand Copper outline
- Never use pure #000000 for large surfaces — use Brand Black (#0C0C0C)

## 3. Typography

### Headline Font
- **Family:** Playfair Display
- **Source:** Google Fonts
- **Weights:** 600 (semibold), 700 (bold), 800 (extrabold)
- **Usage:** Page titles, section headings, logo complement
- **Tracking:** -0.02em for display sizes

### Body Font
- **Family:** Inter
- **Source:** Google Fonts
- **Weights:** 400 (regular), 500 (medium), 600 (semibold)
- **Usage:** Form labels, body text, navigation, data
- **Line height:** 1.5 for body, 1.3 for labels

### Font Pairing Rules
- Never use more than these two families in one view
- Playfair for H1, H2 only; Inter for everything else
- Form labels, buttons, nav items: Always Inter
- Dashboard greeting, page titles: Playfair

## 4. Logo

### Wordmark
- Typeface: Custom decorative serif (Didone style with swashes)
- Text: "Co.Salento"
- Variants: Negro (black), Beige (cream #F0E2C8), Café (brown)

### Symbol
- Two palmas de cera (wax palms) inside an organic coffee-bean-shaped outline
- Represents: Valle de Cocora + coffee origin
- Variants: Negro, Beige

### Logo Placement
- Sidebar header: Symbol (small) + brand name text
- Favicon/PWA icon: Symbol only
- Print/PDF: Full wordmark

### Clear Space
- Minimum clear space: 1x height of the "o" in "Co" on all sides

## 5. Tone of Voice

### Brand Voice
- Warm but professional
- Origin-proud without being nostalgic
- Quality-focused, precise
- Bilingual-ready (Spanish primary, English secondary)

### Use / Avoid

| Use | Avoid |
|-----|-------|
| Artesanal | Industrial |
| Origen | Genérico |
| Trazabilidad | Burocracia |
| Calidad | Perfecto |
| Registro | Papeleo |

## 6. Layout Principles

- Generous whitespace — never cluttered
- Warm backgrounds (cream, not cold white)
- Dark sidebar creates visual anchoring
- Cards with subtle shadows, warm border tones
- Border radius: 12px (cards), 8px (inputs), 20px (badges)

## 7. Output-Specific Overrides

### HTML / PWA (BPM App)
- Sidebar: Brand Black with Brand Beige text
- Active nav: Left border in Brand Copper
- Page bg: Brand Cream (#FAF5ED)
- Card bg: Brand White with warm border (#E8DDD0)
- Buttons primary: Brand Coffee (#6B3A2A)
- Buttons hover: Darken 10%
- Status badges: Use semantic colours with light tint backgrounds
